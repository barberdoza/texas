Texas consolidated Barbershop, Beauty Shop, and Dual Shop licenses into a single "Full-Service Establishment" license type starting in 2024. Older, not-yet-renewed licenses may still carry the legacy labels, which is why both show up here. Addresses are geocoded using the free U.S. Census Bureau batch geocoder rather than published with coordinates, so a portion of shops may not have a mappable location — they're still counted in the totals and table.
